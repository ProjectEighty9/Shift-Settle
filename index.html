    Questions

    Good to know before you book.

    How is my price worked out?

    We look at how much is moving, the access at both ends (stairs, lifts, parking), the distance and your date. You get one price for the whole job before anything is booked.

    What does “Tell your price” mean?

    You tell us what you would like to pay. We say yes, or come back with our closest offer. There is no obligation either way.

    Can you move just one item?

    Yes. Choose “Single item” in the form, or message us on WhatsApp with a photo and we will price it.

    Can I send photos instead of filling in the form?

    Yes. WhatsApp photos of your rooms or items to 07565 021113 and we will use them to price the job.

    Do you move art and antiques?

    Yes. Each piece is packed to suit what it is. Tell us the sizes and any fragile or valuable pieces when you ask for a price.

    Which areas do you cover?

    All of London: north, south, east, west and central. Moving further afield? Ask us and we will tell you.

    How soon can you move me?

    Put your preferred date in the form. We will confirm whether we can do it when we send your price.
